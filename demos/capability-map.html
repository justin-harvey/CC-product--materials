Civic-Chain · product overview

Two ways to run a more open town government

Civic-Chain Procurement runs public purchasing fairly from the first bid to the final payment. The Transparency Ledger publishes your town's financial records so that anyone can check them. Both record their key facts on a public blockchain, so the records can't be quietly changed.

Procurement & solicitations

Buy fairly, award with confidence

Run bids and RFPs from start to finish. Vendors get a simple portal, evaluators score without bias, and every award has a record you can defend.

  • Draft and publish solicitations in minutes
  • Sealed, encrypted bids that no one can read early
  • Blind scoring that holds up to scrutiny
  • Contracts, vendor payments and audit packages in one place
For: procurement officers, purchasing managers, evaluation panels, vendors and the public.
Transparency Ledger (TLaaS)

Show residents the books, and prove they're real

Upload your existing accounting exports and publish them to a public portal. Each one gets a blockchain fingerprint that anyone can check.

  • 23 kinds of financial records, from budgets to bank balances
  • Upload a spreadsheet; no new accounting system needed
  • Automatic checks for errors and personal information
  • One-click verification for residents, auditors and journalists
For: finance directors, town clerks, auditors, councils, residents and the press.

Better together

Use both, and each product puts information into the other automatically.

Contracts meet the budget

Contracts awarded in Procurement appear against the right budget lines in the Transparency Ledger, so finance can see what's committed.

Spending publishes itself

Purchasing-card spending and tax-increment financing (TIF) records go to the public ledger automatically.

One place to verify

Residents check a contract award or a budget on the same public verification page.

Procurement features

Everything from the first draft to the final payment.

Build solicitations fast

Start from a template instead of a blank page.

  • Guided editor for bids and RFPs
  • Ready-made templates and a reusable clause library
  • Split large projects into separately priced lots
  • Compliance requirements suggested automatically
  • Let residents vote on a project first Optional

Reach more vendors

Get more qualified bids with less outreach.

  • Public listing of open opportunities, with a subscribable feed
  • Approved vendors are emailed automatically when a bid opens
  • Invite your own bidder list by email
  • Public notices with a printable affidavit of posting
  • Export in the international OCDS open-contracting standard

Easy vendor onboarding

Vendors sign up once and stay ready to bid.

  • Businesses register themselves and add their staff
  • One vendor account works with many towns
  • Secure upload of insurance and compliance certificates
  • Business identity and bank account verification
  • SAM.gov checks for federally funded work Optional

Secure, fair bidding

Every bidder gets the same information and the same rules.

  • Questions, answers and numbered addenda, with acknowledgements tracked
  • Bids are encrypted on the vendor's own device, so no one, including Civic-Chain, can peek early
  • Late or incomplete bids are blocked automatically
  • See who downloaded the bid package

Blind, defensible evaluation

Decisions based on merit, with a record to prove it.

  • Sealed envelopes opened in a witnessed ceremony
  • Evaluators sign a conflict-of-interest statement first
  • Anonymous scoring against a set rubric
  • Prices stay sealed until technical scores are locked

Award and manage contracts

Flexible contracts with built-in checks.

  • Award one or several vendors, with the reasons on record
  • Pay by milestone, retainer or invoice, or issue task orders under a master contract
  • Contract changes need a second person's approval
  • Keep a backup vendor ready if the winner falls through

Pay vendors Optional add-on

Move from approval to payment without leaving the platform.

  • Funds are set aside when a project is published
  • Vendors submit claims for completed work, and staff approve them
  • Pay by ACH and track each payment to completion
  • Contract spending cards with limits and instant freeze
  • Accounts-payable export, 1099 summary and low-balance alerts

Audit-ready records

Be ready for an audit or a records request at any time.

  • Full history of every solicitation, exportable
  • One-click audit package for any contract
  • Records retention schedules with approved disposal
  • Awards recorded on a public blockchain anyone can check

Transparency Ledger features

Publish your town's finances in a form residents can trust.

Publish your books

Cover the records residents and auditors ask about most.

  • Trial balance, chart of accounts, budgets and general ledger
  • Registers for grants, debt, tax levies, bank balances and more
  • Tax-increment financing (TIF) district reports
  • Purchasing-card spending, published daily

Easy import

Works with the accounting system you already have.

  • Upload a CSV or Excel export
  • A step-by-step wizard previews your data and matches up the columns
  • Save your column mapping and reuse it next period
  • Very large files are processed in the background

Built-in safeguards

Catch mistakes and private data before they go public.

  • Automatic accounting checks before you publish
  • Scans for Social Security numbers and birth dates
  • Your original file is deleted after import
  • Keep any dataset staff-only, with sensitive columns encrypted

Tamper-evident on the blockchain

Prove the numbers haven't changed since you published them.

  • Each dataset gets a digital fingerprint on a public blockchain
  • No money and no raw data ever go on the blockchain
  • Publish from your town's own wallet
  • Choose Polygon, Ethereum, Avalanche, Stellar or Solana

Public transparency portal

Anyone can see the numbers, with no account needed.

  • Browse published records from every participating town
  • See totals and line items, and download the files
  • Check any file against the blockchain in one click
  • Step-by-step instructions for independent auditors and journalists

Fix mistakes the right way

Corrections are transparent instead of hidden.

  • Publish a corrected version that links to the original
  • Withdraw a dataset with a stated reason
  • Deleting records needs two people and produces a certificate

Insights for finance teams

Turn published data into answers.

  • Budget vs actual for the fiscal year
  • Contracts committed against each budget line
  • Purchasing-card spending compared with budget
  • Ask plain-language questions of published records

Control and pricing

The right people publish, and you pay only for what you use.

  • Separate permissions to view, import, export and publish
  • Only the finance team can publish by default
  • Every action is logged
  • Pay per publication, a few cents per dataset per blockchain
