Civic-Chain Product Flow
Product model · Procurement and Transparency Ledger

One product decides the spending. The other proves it.

Procurement is a lifecycle. Each contract moves through it once, from first draft to final payment. The Transparency Ledger (TLaaS) is a loop that publishes the town's books every reporting period. Both write their proof to the same public verification layer.

Procurement: a decision workflow Transparency Ledger: a disclosure loop Shared foundation in both products

What each one is worth

Procurement
An award you can defend

Sealed bids, blind scoring and a second sign-off on contract changes. Any contract produces a single audit package for the auditor.

  • Procurement officers and purchasing managers
  • Evaluation panels and vendors
  • Residents following open bids
Transparency Ledger
Books residents can check themselves

Publish from the accounting system the town already uses. Errors and private data are caught before release, and anyone can verify a file in one click.

  • Finance directors and town clerks
  • Auditors and councils
  • Residents and the press

Better together

A town can buy either product alone. When it runs both, information passes between them automatically.

Procurement → Ledger

Contracts meet the budget

Awarded contracts appear as commitments against the right budget lines, so finance can see what's committed.

Procurement → Ledger

Spending publishes itself

Purchasing-card spending and TIF records flow to the public ledger with no manual upload.

Both → public

One place to verify

Residents check a contract award and a budget on the same verification page.
