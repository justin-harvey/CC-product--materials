Persona 1 · Resident

Dot

Retired teacher, 31 years in town

“I don't want to accuse anyone. I just want to see it.”

GoalKnow her taxes are spent fairly without filing paperwork or accusing anyone.

1Hears a concern
2Looks for the facts
3Reads the contract
4Checks the spending
5Trusts and shares
Before
A neighbor at town meeting says the plow contractor is somebody's cousin.
Files a public records request and waits about three weeks.
Gets meeting minutes, if anything. No record of how the vendor was picked.
Waits for the annual report, months after the money is gone.
Has to take the town's word for it, or keep suspecting.
With Civic-Chain
Same moment, same rumor. What changes is what she can do next.
Opens the public bid listing from her kitchen table. No account needed.
Sees the award, the reasons on record, and that bids were scored blind.
Sees card spending published daily and the contract against its budget line.
Checks the files against their blockchain fingerprints, then shares the link.

Persona 2 · Vendor

Marco

Owns a six-person plowing and paving company

“I'll lose a bid on price. I won't lose one to somebody's cousin.”

GoalWin public work on merit and get paid without chasing invoices.

1Finds the work
2Gets set up
3Submits a bid
4Hears the result
5Gets paid
Before
Checks each town's website and the paper. Misses deadlines.
Fills out paper forms and mails insurance certificates to every town.
Hand-delivers an envelope and wonders who peeks early.
Gets a phone call. Never learns why he lost.
Invoice sits in a pile for weeks with no status.
With Civic-Chain
Gets an email when a matching bid opens, or follows the public feed.
Registers once. One account works with many towns, with certificates uploaded securely.
Encrypts the bid on his own device. Answers to bidder questions are tracked for everyone.
Reads the award and its reasons. Scoring was blind, prices sealed until scores locked.
Claims each milestone, sees it approved, and tracks the ACH to completion.

Persona 3 · Finance director

Ruth

Finance director for a town of 9,000

“Every question from council turns into a week of digging.”

GoalKeep spending inside the budget and answer council, auditors and residents fast.

1Publishes the budget
2Tracks commitments
3Pays vendors
4Closes the month
5Answers questions
Before
Posts a PDF to the website. Nobody can tell if it changed.
Learns about a contract when its first invoice shows up.
Chases approvals and card receipts in envelopes.
Rebuilds reports by hand and redacts SSNs line by line.
Spends weeks pulling files for auditors and records requests.
With Civic-Chain
Uploads the accounting export. Checks confirm it balances, then it publishes with a fingerprint.
Sees each awarded contract appear against the right budget line.
Approves claims, pays by ACH, and sets card limits she can freeze instantly.
Reuses saved column mappings. The privacy scan flags SSNs before anything goes public.
Downloads one audit package per contract. Residents can check the records themselves.
