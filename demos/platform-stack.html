Built from the proof up

Civic-Chain is two products on one foundation. The town keeps the systems it already runs. Every record either product publishes gets a fingerprint on a public blockchain, and the shared safeguards sit between that proof and the products.

Layer 2Public proof

Fingerprints on a public blockchain

Each award, vote and published dataset gets a digital fingerprint that anyone can check. Only the fingerprint goes on the blockchain. No money and no raw data ever do.

Why it's built this way

Nothing to replace

The town keeps its accounting system and its bank. Civic-Chain works from their exports and connections.

Start with either product

Both sit on the same safeguards and proof, so adding the second one later is a module, not a migration.

Proof without exposure

The blockchain holds fingerprints only. Money moves through the town's bank and records stay in Civic-Chain.

Not in the stack yet

Partner APINeither product has API keys for connecting other software.
Prove a single lineThe Ledger can't yet verify one line without the whole dataset.
Bid protestsProcurement has no workflow for filing or deciding a protest.
